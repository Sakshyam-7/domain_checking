My Journey

I entered the insurance industry over 17 years ago with a simple goal: to help people navigate the complexities of financial planning with clarity and honesty.

Over the years, I've seen firsthand how a well-structured life insurance or pension plan can rescue a family from crisis or ensure a peaceful retirement. My philosophy is rooted in needs-based planning—I don't just sell policies; I take the time to understand your unique situation, goals, and fears before recommending a solution.

Today, as a Senior Agency Manager at Nepal Life Insurance Company Ltd, I also dedicate my time to mentoring a new generation of financial advisors, instilling in them the same ethical, client-centered approach that has been the foundation of my career.
